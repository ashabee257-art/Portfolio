About Me

Age 19 · Trivandrum, Kerala

I am Asha B, a Cyber Security student currently pursuing my Cyber Security course at Cyberbee Academy, Trivandrum. My journey into cyber security started with an interest in understanding how networks, systems, websites, and digital applications work and how they can be protected from security threats.

I come from a Commerce background and am currently developing my technical skills in cyber security through hands-on learning and practical projects. I am particularly interested in ethical hacking, penetration testing, network security, vulnerability assessment, threat detection, incident response, and system security.

I enjoy learning by building practical projects rather than only studying theory. I am continuously improving my Python fundamentals and cybersecurity knowledge while gaining practical exposure to tools such as Nmap, Wireshark, Burp Suite, and Hydra, along with TCP/IP networking concepts.

My goal is to continue developing strong technical and problem-solving skills and build a career in cyber security, ethical hacking, and defensive security.
